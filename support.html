Here to help

How can we help?

Support for your food, workouts, water, sleep and weight journal.

Contact FORGE support

Email ForgeSupp@proton.me.

To help us understand an issue, include your app version (in Settings), your Windows or phone OS version, the steps that led to the problem, and the exact error message. A screenshot with private information hidden can help.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, purchase tokens, secret keys, journal backups or private health photos.

Install or update FORGE on Windows

  1. Open the FORGE Microsoft Store listing. Availability depends on the listing's publication status and your region.
  2. Use Microsoft Store to install FORGE, or check its Library for available updates.
  3. If an installation or update fails, note the Store error code, restart Microsoft Store, and try again. For Store download or payment problems, visit Microsoft Store support.

Purchases and subscriptions

The Windows version uses Microsoft Store subscriptions. Use the same Microsoft account that made your purchase and try the restore-purchases option in FORGE's Settings if your plan is not recognized.

Manage recurring billing and cancellation at Microsoft account services and subscriptions. For Android or iOS purchases, use the subscription settings in the store where you purchased.

Uninstalling FORGE does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests are handled by the store where you paid. If a restore fails, send support the error message rather than your receipt or purchase token.

Back up your journal before reinstalling

Your journal is stored locally on your device. Before uninstalling, resetting Windows, clearing app data or moving devices, use the backup/export option in Settings and save the file somewhere you control.

Use the restore option in Settings to import a FORGE backup. Read the replacement warning before confirming. Keep backup files private because they can contain your health information.

If data fails to load, try the app's recovery or retry action and contact support before clearing anything. We cannot recover a local journal from a server copy.

AI, barcode and connection problems

  • AI features need your consent in Settings and an internet connection. Check the app's displayed allowance and error message.
  • If a service is temporarily unavailable, try again later. Barcode coverage varies; you can enter a food manually if a product is missing.
  • Check estimated calories, portions and nutrients before saving. AI output is an estimate, not medical advice.

Goals, reminders and permissions

You can change goals from Home and reminder preferences in Settings. If reminders do not appear, check Windows or phone notification permissions and quiet/focus settings.

Camera access is used for supported photo and scanning features. Health-data integration is platform-dependent; Windows does not provide the Android or iOS health-import features.
