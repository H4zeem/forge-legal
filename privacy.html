Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

FORGE is developed by H4zem. This policy covers the Windows, Android and iOS app and the server features we operate. Your journal is stored on your device. Selected online features send limited information as described below. FORGE has no separate email/password account.

1. Information stored on your device

Food entries, workouts, water, sleep, weight, profile details, goals, preferences, reminder settings, recent searches and cached reference results are stored locally. FORGE does not automatically upload your complete journal to our server or provide a hosted journal backup.

Some local profile and journal information is health-related. Device security, operating-system backup settings and any files you export affect who can access it. Local storage and exported JSON backups are not encrypted by a separate FORGE passphrase. Use a device lock and keep exported files private.

2. When information leaves your device

Optional AI features

Before using AI, FORGE asks for your consent. Photo analysis sends the selected food photo; AI food search sends your query; meal planning sends calorie and macro goals and the preferences you enter. Requests can include your requested language and technical app information. They pass through our Cloudflare Worker to Google Gemini. Do not include information you do not want processed by these services.

The app removes photo metadata, such as location and camera information, before sending supported images. Our Worker does not save AI photos, prompts or results as a hosted journal. Google processes requests under its own service terms and retention rules; this is not a promise of zero retention by Google. Review Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy. AI consent can be withdrawn in Settings for future requests; withdrawal does not undo processing already completed.

Barcode lookup

Barcode lookup sends the product barcode through our server to Open Food Facts. Product reference results may be cached locally and on our server. A barcode lookup does not upload your complete meal log or consumption history. See Open Food Facts privacy information.

Subscriptions and purchase verification

Purchases and payments are handled by Microsoft Store, Google Play or Apple's App Store, depending on platform. The app and our server process Store purchase proofs, subscription status, product identifiers and a random installation/device identifier to verify access, restore purchases, handle payment problems and enforce plan allowances. Windows verification also involves Microsoft's Store services. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Server-cached purchase proofs are encrypted; entitlement information is also cached on your device.

Security and service operation

Online features process your random device identifier or a hash derived from it, IP/network information, request timestamps/signatures, session tokens, app version and security or purchase-verification results. Android uses Google Play Integrity; iOS uses Apple App Attest/DeviceCheck where available. Windows access is tied to server-verified Microsoft Store purchases. These checks help prevent replay, fraud, quota abuse and unauthorized access. They are not a separate FORGE login.

Daily allowance, rewarded-ad claim and abuse-prevention records are kept to operate those features. A hashed installation identifier may be sent to AdMob to verify a rewarded-ad credit. It is still an identifier, not a guarantee of anonymity.

3. Health Connect and Apple Health

On supported mobile devices, you can grant access to read weight and sleep from Health Connect or Apple Health. Imported records are added to your local journal. This integration does not upload the imported health-record collection to our server, write records back to those health services, or use imported health records for advertising. Windows does not provide these mobile health imports.

AI requests are separate and may contain the goals, preferences or content you choose to send as described above. You can revoke health access through your operating-system settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete records already imported; use FORGE's data controls to remove local information.

4. Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

On supported Android/iOS free-plan features, Google AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and consent signals under Google's privacy policy. FORGE uses Google's consent/privacy-options flow where available. Options depend on your region, device and the ad configuration. Paid plans do not show ads. The Windows app does not integrate AdMob advertising.

Subscriptions use native Store services. FORGE has no custom usage-analytics upload service or analytics opt-in toggle, and does not send journal contents to a dedicated analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Development builds can produce local diagnostics. Our Worker logs controlled error categories/status information rather than AI content or purchase proofs; hosting and Store providers may separately process operational/security data under their own policies.

5. Support, backups and sharing

If you email ForgeSupp@proton.me, we receive your address and what you send. This mailbox uses Proton; see Proton's privacy information. Please do not email passwords, purchase tokens, journal backups or private health photos. Support correspondence is used to answer requests and resolve issues, not for marketing.

When you export a backup or share a progress card, the selected information is written to a file and passed to the destination you choose. Backups can contain your journal/profile data and are plaintext. FORGE attempts to remove its temporary share copies, but copies saved by you, a receiving app or the operating system are outside its control.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local journal: remains until you remove entries, clear local app data or uninstall, subject to operating-system behavior and copies you keep. Export a backup before destructive actions; we cannot recover a journal from a hosted copy.
  • Server records: expire according to their purpose. Daily quota records generally expire after about a day; product caches commonly last up to seven days. Device/security records can remain for approximately 180–400 days, and cached purchase records for up to 400 days after their most recent write. Active use or renewed verification can refresh these periods.
  • Identity and purchases: clearing the journal is not a server-data deletion request and does not reset all identity, anti-abuse or purchase state. An iOS Keychain identifier may survive reinstalling. Stores and service providers retain their own records under their policies and applicable obligations.
  • Support and user-created copies: support correspondence may remain as needed to resolve requests, maintain records and meet applicable obligations. Backups or shared copies remain wherever you saved or sent them until removed there.

For access, correction or deletion requests concerning server/support information, contact us. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy, withdraw consent or complain to a data-protection authority. We may need enough information to verify a request; do not send secrets or private health records as identity proof. Legal obligations or proportionate fraud prevention may limit deletion.

7. Providers, international processing and security

The providers described above include Cloudflare, Google, Apple, Microsoft, Open Food Facts and Proton. They may process data outside your country. Their services and privacy terms also apply to their processing. We use the information described here to deliver requested features, administer purchases, protect the service and answer support requests; consent is used for optional features where applicable. We do not sell your journal or use imported health records for targeted advertising.

Connections to our server use HTTPS, signed requests and server-side verification. These safeguards reduce risk but do not make any app or transmission completely secure.

8. Children

FORGE is not intended for children under 13, or below a higher minimum age required where they live. We do not knowingly collect children's information through our online features. If you believe a child supplied information, contact us so we can investigate.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its providers change. The effective date identifies the current version. For questions or privacy requests, contact H4zem at ForgeSupp@proton.me.
